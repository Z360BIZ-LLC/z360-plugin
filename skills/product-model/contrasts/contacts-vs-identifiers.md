# Contacts vs identifiers

A **contact** is the durable identity record for an external person or business.

An **identifier** is a value that can locate or reach that contact, such as a name, email address, phone number, tag, or linked external ID.

## Boundary

A contact can have several identifiers, and an identifier can change, be shared, or match more than one person. An identifier helps establish identity; it does not replace the contact record or prove that a match is unique.
