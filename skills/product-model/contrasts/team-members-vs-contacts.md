# Team members vs contacts

A **team member** is an internal organization user who can own, receive, or manage workspace work.

A **contact** is an external person or business the organization communicates with.

## Boundary

The same real-world person can be represented in both systems, but internal assignment and access use the team-member concept while customer identity and communication use the contact concept.
