# Tickets vs notes

A **ticket** represents accountable work with an owner, status, or a lifecycle that can outlive the conversation.

A **note** preserves context or records what happened without assigning work.

## Boundary

Both can sit on the same conversation, but a note does not become a work queue and a ticket does not merely record history.
