# Work Items vs conversations

A **Work Item** in Work mode records progress toward one pipeline goal. It can involve several stakeholders and link several Inbox conversations.

An **Inbox conversation** records communication with a contact through one channel. It holds the messages and activity for that exchange. The same conversation can be linked to more than one Work Item.

## Boundary

The Work Item records which pipeline step comes next and whether the goal is complete. The Inbox conversation shows what was said or sent. A contact's reply may complete the Work Item if it meets the goal's criteria; a queued send alone does not establish that outcome.
