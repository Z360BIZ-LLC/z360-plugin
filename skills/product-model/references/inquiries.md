# Inquiries and leads

An inquiry is the product record commonly described to users as a lead. The two terms refer to the same customer-lifecycle concept. An inquiry represents a potential or existing customer moving through a pipeline, rather than merely identifying a contact or recording a conversation.

An inquiry can relate to a contact and conversation and carries lifecycle context such as pipeline, stage, source, status, custom fields, and ownership. The contact answers “who is this?”; the inquiry answers “where is this customer in the business lifecycle?”.

An inquiry can be attached to a conversation as a composition message. A conversation can contain more than one business record, but the lead lifecycle itself remains an inquiry concept. Only one inquiry can be active in a conversation at a time; another competing inquiry may be idle. Pipeline and status semantics belong with the [pipeline](pipelines.md) model.

An inquiry is not a ticket: a ticket tracks work to resolve, while an inquiry tracks customer progression. It is also not a note or reminder, which preserve context or prompt teammate action without representing a customer lifecycle.
