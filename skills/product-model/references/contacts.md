# Contacts

A contact is a person or business that the organization communicates with. It is the identity record for the external party, not a conversation, a ticket, or an inquiry. A contact may be identified by a name, email address, phone number, tags, linked identifiers, company relationship, and custom fields.

Contacts can have multiple conversations and can be linked to inquiries and other business records. The contact supplies identity and relationship context; the linked records describe interactions, customer lifecycle, or work connected to that identity.

Do not treat an email address, phone number, or conversation as a replacement for the contact record. Those values can identify or reach a contact, but the contact is the durable representation of the person or business in the organization.

Contacts are external parties even when an internal user happens to be the same person in the real world. Internal ownership, assignment, and access belong to [team members](team-members.md), not to the contact model.
