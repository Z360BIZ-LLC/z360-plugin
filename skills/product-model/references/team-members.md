# Team members

A team member is an internal user of the organization workspace. Team members may own work, receive reminders, manage records, configure workspace settings, or share responsibility for an organization-owned resource.

Team-member concepts include identity, role, visibility, ownership, assignment, and organization membership. The current user is the person acting in the workspace; another team member may be the owner or assignee of a record.

Team members are not contacts. Contacts represent external people or businesses that the organization communicates with, while team members represent internal workspace participants. The same real-world person could appear in both systems, but one record must not be substituted for the other.
