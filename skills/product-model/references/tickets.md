# Tickets

A ticket is a durable work record. It gives an issue or request an identifiable place for ownership, status, priority, type, and follow-up. A ticket can remain meaningful after the originating conversation has gone quiet, the original owner has changed, or another teammate has picked up the work.

A ticket can be linked to a contact and attached to a conversation as a composition message. Several tickets can belong to one conversation when the thread contains several independent pieces of work. The conversation provides interaction history; the ticket provides the work boundary.

The ticket concept applies to work that needs an accountable owner, a visible lifecycle, or continuity beyond the current exchange. A ticket is not a customer lifecycle record, a context-only note, a time-bound teammate prompt, or a message.
